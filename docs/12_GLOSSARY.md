
# Glossary

## Minimum Amount Due (MAD)

Minimum amount payable by the cardholder by the payment due date, as calculated by the issuer under applicable terms.

## Total Amount Due

Entire amount payable on the credit-card statement.

## Statement Balance

Balance displayed on the credit-card statement.

## Finance Charge

Interest charged on an outstanding credit-card balance.

## Annual Interest Rate

Annualized rate used to calculate finance charges.

## Billing Period

Period covered by the illustrative calculation.

## Day Count Basis

Denominator used to convert annual interest into a period rate, such as 365.

## Late-Payment Fee

Fee that may apply when payment remains overdue under issuer terms and applicable regulations.

## GST

Goods and Services Tax.

## Interest-Bearing Principal

Amount assumed to attract interest in the simulator.

## Illustrative Estimate

A calculation based on disclosed assumptions. It is not a confirmed bank charge.

## Credit Information Company

An organization that maintains credit information and provides credit reports under applicable law.
