How minimum payments affect interest
  • Interest-free period: If it is lost, interest may apply from transaction dates, depending on issuer terms.
  • Different rates: Retail purchases, cash advances, and other transaction types may have different APRs.
  • Over time: If a balance carries over between billing cycles, interest may compound under issuer terms.
  • Illustrative minimum: The default 5% is an illustrative setting; issuers set their own minimum-due terms.

Assumptions: selected interest period, a 365-day year, no new transactions, and no interest on tax or fees. GST uses an illustrative 18% rate. Full payment shows no new illustrative interest; previously accrued or unbilled amounts are not modeled.

RBI past-due timing and illustrative fees

RBI rules allow an issuer to report an account as past due or apply penal charges after it has remained overdue for more than three days. This does not guarantee a fee-free period of three days. Late fees depend on issuer terms and the post-due outstanding; the listed options are illustrative, not RBI-prescribed.
